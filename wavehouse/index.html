Overview

During the COVID-19 pandemic, Wave House faced the challenge of increasing bookings while simultaneously coordinating the individuals responsible for delivering the guest experience.

To address this, Maksim consolidated marketing and day-to-day operations into a unified system, encompassing all aspects from customer acquisition and bookings to administrators, surf instructors, drivers, and on-site execution.
