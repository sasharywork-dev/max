Transforming LATAM Market Complexity into a P2P Growth Strategy

Overview

In a region processing over $730 billion in annual digital asset volume, Binance P2P held a commanding product advantage across Latin America and the Caribbean. However, sharp economic disparities ranging from Argentina and Venezuela's inflation-driven stablecoin demand to Brazil's institutional scale made a blanket regional playbook impossible.

To address this, Maksim engineered a localized Growth Marketing Framework, establishing dedicated market prioritization, localized user positioning, and custom friction-free acquisition paths that transformed regional variance into a structured growth driver.

Role

Managed the strategic work from:

- Positioning;
- Market Analysis;
- ICP Development;
- Country Prioritisation;
- Competitor Intelligence;
- Customer Journey Map;
- Regional Acquisition Framework.

Product marketing manager · pmm

Problem

Binance P2P possessed significant structural advantages, including global liquidity, localized payment systems, zero-fee retail trading, and escrow infrastructure. However, LATAM could not be considered a homogeneous market.


The challenge was to identify the strategic focus areas for Binance P2P, determine the key stakeholders to prioritize, ascertain the specific needs of each segment, and devise a localized positioning strategy for a global product.

Solution

I

Prioritize Market Opportunities

Analyzed regional adoption, economic drivers, local payment infrastructure, peer-to-peer use cases, and market potential to transform Latin America into a prioritized expansion framework.

II

ICP Segmentation

Translated the broad regional demand into four distinct customer profiles,mapping their objectives, challenges, search behavior, content preferences, objections, and conversion triggers, with a concise preview of each detailed below.

Market & Segment

III

Examine the Competitive Environment

Conducted a comprehensive analysis of competitors across all six priority markets.

This analysis integrated quantitative data sourced from Ahrefs, Semrush, PageSpeed Insights, and Google Trends with qualitative analysis encompassing product, pricing, customer segments, positioning, monetization strategies, and market gaps.

IV

Develop Hyper-Personalized Positioning System

Translated market insights and competitive analysis into a localized positioning strategy, customized to the economic context, user requirements, and competitive dynamics of each market.
