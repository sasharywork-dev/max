CryptoRobotics

Enhancing the Organic Market Reach of a Cryptocurrency Trading Platform

Overview

Although CryptoRobotics already enjoyed significant organic visibility, had a small market share in the projected $38.5 billion hyper-growth cryptocurrency trading platforms market, Maksim developed a scalable Search-Growth System to capture the influx of 740 million global cryptocurrency owners transitioning to automated systems.

The framework aggressively expanded their market reach across over 15 interconnected exchanges, establishing a programmatic customer acquisition engine to capture the surging retail demand for algorithmic and copy-trading tools.

Role

Maksim demonstrates strategic foresight and actively engages in the execution process across various domains, including:

- Off/On-Page Analysis;
- Internal Linkbuilding;
- Keyword Clustering;
- Content Creation;
- KPI Optimization;
- GEO & AEO;
- Localization.

[2]

SEO Growth Manager · AI Search

Problem

Unlike a website commencing from scratch, CryptoRobotics possessed an established organic presence.

From March to September 2024, the website achieved the following metrics:
~ 22,300 organic clicks
~ 996,000 impressions
~ An average position of 25.1

Afterwards, the challenge shifted to identifying the next level of growth without solely relying on incremental ranking enhancements to the existing search visibility.

The opportunity lay in identifying demand that the website was not yet capturing, systematically expanding its topical coverage, and transforming that additional search surface into user acquisition.

Solution

This eight-step framework represents an advanced, growth-oriented organic search strategy tailored specifically for high-volatility, high-stakes industries such as cryptocurrency, Web3, and algorithmic trading.

Integrating traditional search engine optimization with sophisticated AI discovery architecture, Your Money or Your Life (YMYL) trust frameworks, and aggressive conversion rate optimization, this methodology transforms SEO from a passive traffic-generation tool into a resilient, omnichannel user acquisition engine.
