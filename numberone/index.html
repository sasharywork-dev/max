Turning Paid Media Into a Predictable Sales Channel

Overview

The primary objective was to transform Facebook and Instagram from engagement channels into a consistent source of sales.

Role

From August 2019 to July 2021, successfully managed paid acquisition campaigns across Facebook and Instagram platforms. Throughout this period, Maksim consistently evaluated and optimized targeting strategies, creative content, campaign objectives, and budget allocation to enhance lead generation and sales performance.

PAID MEDIA MANAGER

Problem

[2]

Fashion content can generate reach, clicks, and engagement without necessarily generating customers.

Over time, traditional traffic campaigns and interest-based targeting became less effective, while audiences demonstrating stronger purchase intent through Instagram Direct Messaging proved more valuable.

Solution

I

Test What Actually Drives Demand

Continuously tested audiences, creatives, placements, campaign objectives, and product-focused content across Facebook and Instagram.


Video content helped showcase Number 1’s inventory, while campaign performance determined which combinations of creative, audience, and objective deserved additional investment.

II

Optimize for Intent, Not Traffic

As traffic-focused campaigns became less effective, Maksim shifted acquisition toward Instagram Direct Messaging, where customer behavior demonstrated stronger commercial intent.


Instead of optimizing primarily for clicks, campaigns increasingly focused on starting conversations with potential buyers.

III

Build Audiences From Real Customer Behavior

Used engagement and messaging data to build and refine audiences based on people already interacting with the brand.


Importantly, Maksim separated broad engagement from stronger buying signals, continuously adjusting targeting based on which audiences generated meaningful inquiries and sales.

IV

Scale What Converted

Continuously reallocated budget toward the campaign structures producing qualified conversations and sales, while maintaining a smaller share for product visibility and new audience acquisition.


This created a repeatable feedback loop:

Campaign → Customer Signal → Better Audience → Qualified Lead → Sale → Reinvestment
